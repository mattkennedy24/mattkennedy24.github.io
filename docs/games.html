      How scoring works

      Each result becomes a score from 0 to 100. A day’s score is the average of the games scored that day, and the average above is the mean of your daily scores. A streak counts every day with at least one game logged, and today doesn’t break it until the day is over. If a result prints its own date, like Contexto, it is filed under that date instead of the one you picked.

      Your results are saved only in this browser. Use Export and Import to move them to another device.
