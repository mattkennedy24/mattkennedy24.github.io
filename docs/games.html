      How scoring works

      Each result becomes a base score from 0 to 100, using the rules below. A ★ result is an exceptional one: it counts double in the day’s Average and adds 10 points to the day’s Total. The Average is the weighted mean of the day’s scores, and the Total adds them up. Your overall Average is the mean of your daily Averages, and your overall Total is the sum of your daily Totals. Clues by Sam is link-only and isn’t scored.

      This is version one of the scoring, and I’ll keep tuning it. Only your pasted results are saved, so your history re-scores automatically when the rules change. They live in this browser only; use Export and Import to move them to another device.
